# What Compost Actually Is
**Compost = Biology**
- Microbes  
- Fungi  
- Worms  
- Organic matter  
- Time + moisture + air  

---

# The 3‑Part Compost Formula
**Browns + Greens + Air/Moisture**
- Browns = carbon (leaves, cardboard, wood chips)  
- Greens = nitrogen (food scraps, coffee grounds)  
- Air = structure  
- Moisture = life  

---

# Why Coffee Grounds Are Magic
- High nitrogen  
- Fine particle size  
- Microbial booster  
- Worm favorite  
- Great for fungal activity  
- Smells amazing when composting  

---

# How to Use Coffee Grounds Safely
- Mix with browns  
- Avoid compaction  
- Add in thin layers  
- Combine with leaves or cardboard  
- Perfect for worm bins  
- Great for soil structure

# How to Start a Compost Pile at Home
**Simple Steps**
1. Pick a spot  
2. Add browns  
3. Add greens  
4. Add water  
5. Mix weekly  
6. Add coffee grounds  
7. Add worms (optional)  

---

# Troubleshooting
- **If it smells:** add browns  
- **If it’s dry:** add water  
- **If it’s wet:** add browns + mix  
- **If it’s slow:** add greens  
- **If pests show up:** cover food scraps
